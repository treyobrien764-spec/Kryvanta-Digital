06 — QUESTIONS ANSWERED

THE DETAILS,
MADE SIMPLE.

Everything you should know before we build your new site.

ASK US A QUESTION ↗
Do I need to pay every month for a website? +

No. Our build packages are one-time development fees. Hosting is separate. You can arrange hosting yourself or select a monthly plan.

Do you design websites for businesses outside Indiana? +

Absolutely. We can work remotely with businesses across the United States.

Will the website work on mobile phones? +

Yes. Every package includes responsive layouts designed for phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Can I request extra pages or booking features? +

Yes. We can quote extra pages, scheduling integrations, and custom functionality after we review the requirements.

Do I own my domain? +

We recommend registering your domain in your own name and keeping control of it. Website source ownership and handoff terms will be specified in the project agreement.

How soon can my website be ready? +

We'll confirm a realistic completion timeline once we know the number of pages, content readiness, and current workload.
